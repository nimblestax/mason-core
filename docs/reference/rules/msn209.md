## What it checks

A Gold Model reads another Domain's gold, or silver that Domain hasn't Exported.

## Why

Each Domain owns its tables, and only the tables it publishes are read by other Domains. A Domain publishes silver by Exporting it, with `export: true`, and its gold and the rest of its silver stay its own, free to change. The Shared Domain, the Domain called `shared`, holds what every Domain reads, so its gold is the one gold other Domains may read. Reads that only point this way can't form a loop between Domains.

## Example

Write these files into the project `mason init` creates, and `mason validate` fails:

`mason.platform.yaml`:

```yaml
kind: platform
version: 1
name: shop
environments:
  dev:
    host: https://your-workspace.cloud.databricks.com
naming:
  catalog: "{env}_{domain}"
  schema: "{layer}"
  layers:
    sources: [bronze, silver]
    models: [gold]
domains:
  - sales
  - finance
```

`domains/finance/sources/invoices.yaml`:

```yaml
kind: source
version: 1
path: /Volumes/{env}_finance/bronze/landing/invoices
format: json
```

`domains/sales/models/gold/fct_orders.sql`:

```sql
SELECT order_id, amount
FROM finance.silver.invoices
```

## Fix

Read silver the other Domain has Exported, or ask its owner to Export it. To share a Gold Model, move it into the Shared Domain.

## Principle

This Rule enforces [Data flows one way](https://nimblestax.com/way/data-flows-one-way), from the Nimblestax Way.
